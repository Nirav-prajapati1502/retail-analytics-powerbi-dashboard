# 🛍️ RetailCo Multi-Channel Retail Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard showcasing retail sales, profitability, promotions, customer behavior, inventory management, and regional business performance through data cleaning, modeling, and visualization.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes multi-channel retail business data using Microsoft Power BI.  
It includes data cleaning, transformation, star schema modeling, KPI creation, and interactive dashboard development to generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps analyze:
- Sales and profitability performance across regions and channels
- Product category and subcategory contribution
- Store and regional operational efficiency
- Promotion and discount effectiveness
- Customer retention and return behavior
- Inventory and supply chain health

# 🔑 Key Insights

- Executive Overview: Total sales reached ₹1.26M with ₹386.94K total profit and 60K total orders generated across all regions.
- Channel Analysis: In-Store sales contributed over 81% of total revenue compared to online sales channels.
- Product Performance: Electronics Accessories emerged as the highest revenue-generating category contributing nearly 45% of overall sales.
- Regional Insights: North region generated the highest revenue contribution while Kolkata became the best-performing city.
- Promotion Analysis: Promotional campaigns achieved a strong ROI of 8.11 with an average discount rate of 18.72%.
- Customer Insights: Customer retention rate remained exceptionally high at 99.98% with a low overall return rate of 1.82%.
- Inventory Health: Total stock availability exceeded ₹12M while identifying 150 low-stock products requiring replenishment actions.
